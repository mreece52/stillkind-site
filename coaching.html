Good To Know

Coaching isn't therapy.

I'm a coach, not a therapist, counselor or doctor. Coaching is about where you want to go and the next step to get there. It works alongside therapy, never instead of it.

Coaching is for

  • Getting unstuck and making a real plan
  • Habits and routines you can actually keep
  • Career moves, especially out of hospitality
  • Leading people in a more human way

Coaching is not

  • Diagnosis or treatment of any mental health condition
  • A replacement for therapy, medication or medical care
  • Addiction treatment
  • Crisis support

If therapy would serve you better, I'll tell you straight and help you find someone. Plenty of people do both.

If you're in crisis or thinking about suicide, please don't wait for an intro call. Call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, any time. In an emergency, call 911.
